Authentic Craftsmanship

Four Decades of Purity, Passion & Royal Flavour

♦

Founded in 1984 in the cultural heart of Lahore, Sweet Salim was born from a singular conviction: that culinary art should never compromise on purity.

We source single-origin cardamom from Kerala, high-grade saffron from Kashmir, and churn our khoya from whole buffalo milk daily. Every recipe is a tribute to royal Mughal and Punjabi heritage.
