A Heritage of Purity

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted with Reverence

♦

In the autumn of 1984, Master Confectioner Salim started with a singular, unbending philosophy: that sweets are not merely food, but an expression of love, honor, and celebration.

While commercial confectioneries shifted to automated mass production and vegetable oils, Sweet Salim remained faithful to time-honored artisanal methods. We still simmer our pure milk khoya in thick brass cauldrons and fry our motichoor drops in 100% clarified butter (desi ghee).

Over the decades, our kitchen expanded from classical mithai into royal Mughlai and Lahori cuisine—bringing that same obsession with unadulterated ingredients to slow-cooked mutton biryanis, dum karahis, and bespoke celebratory cakes.
